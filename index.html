Bibbit Support

Need help with Bibbit? Email app@zarefoss.com and include your device model, iOS version, and what you were trying to do.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need an account?

No. Bibbit has no accounts. It uses the Apple Account you're already signed in with to sync through iCloud.

How do I share with my partner, nanny, or a grandparent?

Open Settings → Household → Invite, then send the invite by Messages, Mail, or AirDrop. They need an iPhone or iPad with Bibbit installed and an Apple Account signed in to iCloud. You choose whether each person can edit or only view.

My partner's entries aren't showing up.

Make sure both of you are signed in to iCloud, have a network connection, and have accepted the same household invite. Changes usually appear within seconds. If they don't, open the app on both devices to prompt a sync.

Someone in my family doesn't have an iPhone. How do I share with them?

Use Export to create a PDF or CSV and send it by email or Messages. These open on any device.

How do I send a log to my child's doctor?

Use Export, choose the child, date range, and categories, and send the PDF. It includes charts, daily totals, and a time-stamped log.

Can I change the options, like poop colors or sizes?

Yes. Go to Settings → Categories, choose a category, and add, rename, hide, or reorder its options. You can also add your own categories and attributes.

I deleted an entry by mistake.

Open Settings → Recently Deleted and tap Restore. Deleted entries are kept for 30 days.

Does Bibbit tell me how much medicine to give or when?

No. Bibbit records what was given and when, so you can look back. Always follow your doctor's instructions and the medication label. You can set your own reminder after logging a dose.

Why don't I see the type-or-talk feature?

Natural-language entry needs a device that supports Apple Intelligence, running iOS 26 or later, with Apple Intelligence turned on. On other devices, the big buttons, Siri shortcuts, and Control Center controls work the same.

What happens to my data if I delete the app?

Your data stays in your iCloud account and returns when you reinstall. To remove it completely, see "Deleting your data" in the Privacy Policy.

Is my data private?

Yes. Nothing is sent to the developer. See the Privacy Policy for details.
